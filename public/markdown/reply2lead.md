# Reply2Lead — AI-Assisted Lead Conversion

Reply2Lead receives social messages, qualifies leads, grounds suggested replies in business knowledge, automates responses, and supports human takeover across multiple shops.

![Reply2Lead architecture schematic](/img/projects/arch-reply2lead.svg)

*Simplified architecture schematic.*

## Contributions

- Built asynchronous, claim-gated AI autopilot orchestration with deduplication and conversation locking.
- Added shop-scoped AI identities and tenant-isolated access paths.
- Implemented social-channel OAuth and page-identity persistence.
- Contributed cross-shop administration, knowledge visibility, deployment configuration, and CI hardening.

## Stack

Kotlin, Micronaut, Arrow, PostgreSQL, pgvector, PostGIS, asynchronous events and jobs, S3-compatible storage, React, TypeScript, TanStack Query, and Zustand.
